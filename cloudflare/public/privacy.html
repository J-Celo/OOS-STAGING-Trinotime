Privacy notice

How TrinoTime QC Tracker processes authorized quality-control work activity, account and shift information, and productivity metrics. This notice covers the Chrome extension, including the production version and the TrinoTime service used for synchronization.

What it does

Observes supported Augmoto Coverage and Confirmation work interactions to provide QC counters, shift status, and authorized TrinoTime reporting.

Where it operates

The extension is limited to the approved Augmoto labeling site and the configured TrinoTime site. The current beta uses a separate staging service.

1. Scope and responsible publisher

TrinoTime QC Tracker is operated by TrinoTime for authorized workplace quality-control tracking. Only users who have been authorized by their organization should use the extension. It is not an official Augmoto product or a replacement for Augmoto's own records.

The staging extension connects to TrinoTime STAGING QA, the staging Tracker backend. The policy is hosted at the TrinoTime domain for transparency.

2. Information we handle

Depending on the workflow and user session, the extension may process:

  • Work account and affiliation: TrinoTime account identifiers, display names, authorized POD/team assignments, shift identity, and device/session pairing state.
  • QC activity: Coverage/Confirmation workflow type, job IDs, task numbers, task action categories, submission and verification signals, observed store names, work timing, active/idle/away time, and timestamps.
  • Work-related page content: limited text, task context, page identifiers, and URL information needed to recognize supported task and job screens. The extension is not intended to monitor unrelated browsing.
  • Local recovery/diagnostics: pending sync records, submission checkpoints, unresolved/QA-review entries, Tracker settings, and error/connection statuses.
  • Authentication and instructions: temporary account-link/session tokens and POD-specific instructions delivered through TrinoChat.

Some information, such as an employee's name or identifiable workplace activity, may be personal data even when the organization originally created the account.

3. Why the information is used

Data is used to provide the Tracker's stated purpose: task-level QC productivity counters, submission-status and QA review, shift/session continuity, active/idle/away measurements, controlled synchronization to TrinoTime, and delivery of authorized store assignments and Team Leader/Admin instructions. Records may support an organization's permitted workforce reporting and supervision.

Tracking is scoped to relevant Augmoto work pages and the configured TrinoTime portal. The Tracker is not designed for general advertising profiles or monitoring unrelated personal browsing.

4. Storage and service processing

Some data stays in the browser's extension storage or supported work-tab session storage so the Tracker can resume after popup closure, navigation, or temporary network interruptions. Local data may include counters, job/task records, pairing information, and pending or unverified submissions.

When the authorized account and network connection permit, the extension sends relevant productivity, pairing, and shift-sync information over HTTPS to a TrinoTime service running on Cloudflare Workers. TrinoTime uses its configured backend services, including Google Firebase where applicable, to process account/workforce records. Provider infrastructure may also process operational request logs.

The production endpoint is oos.trinotime.com. The extension does not need permission to access every website.

5. Who may receive or access information

  • Authorized organization staff: permitted Team Leaders and Administrators may see workforce metrics, shift/status information, or POD-scoped instruction and store information in TrinoTime according to their access rights.
  • Service providers: Cloudflare provides web/Worker delivery and associated infrastructure, and Google Firebase may handle authenticated workforce or identity data, subject to the deployed system configuration.
  • Required disclosures: information may be disclosed where required by applicable law or to investigate security incidents, subject to legal obligations.

TrinoTime does not sell employee activity, productivity data, or personal information to advertisers, data brokers, or other companies. Access by authorized staff and processing by service providers to operate the service are described above and are not a sale of that data.

6. Retention, correction, and deletion

On-device data

The browser extension may keep local productivity counters, job/task checkpoints, pending synchronization records, pairing state, and QA diagnostics until those data are changed or removed by supported Tracker or browser actions. There is no verified automatic time-based deletion schedule covering all local records. Clearing browser data or removing the extension may delete local information, including work that has not yet synchronized. Before making such changes, users should check pending work with their authorized administrator.

TrinoTime service records

Employee activity, attendance, shift, and Tracker productivity records stored in TrinoTime are retained until an authorized TrinoTime Administrator manually deletes them. TrinoTime does not currently apply an automatic time-based deletion schedule to these service records. Deleting a record from the active service does not by itself erase separate browser-local copies or any independently retained logs and backups.

Backups and operational logs

The existence and retention schedules of automatic Firebase or Cloudflare backups have not been verified by the publisher, and no fixed backup retention period is promised. Where operational logs or backup copies exist, they may follow separate service-provider or administrator-controlled retention and deletion processes. Removal from the live database may not immediately remove older copies. The contact below can be used to ask what copies are known to exist and how a request is handled.

Requests and record accuracy

To ask about your data, request access or corrections, or request deletion, email jcelo@trinotime.com and identify the relevant TrinoTime account or shift without sending passwords. The publisher or an authorized administrator will review the request, verify identity and workplace authorization as needed, and determine whether primary service records can be corrected or deleted, subject to applicable employment, payroll, legal, and security obligations. Separate local browser copies and any retained backups or logs may require additional action. This process and support mailbox must be confirmed operational before publication.

Uninstalling or changing an extension ID can make locally stored unsynchronized data unavailable. Users should contact their authorized administrator before removing an installed Tracker that may contain outstanding work.

7. Safeguards

The current beta limits extension host access to the supported Augmoto site and the TrinoTime staging domain, uses HTTPS for service communication, and relies on authenticated device/account linking and server-side access controls. No networked system can guarantee absolute security; report suspected issues to the contact below.

8. Your choices and workplace notice

Your organization should explain the workplace tracking arrangement and establish an appropriate basis for processing where required. Depending on applicable law and employment requirements, you may ask about your records, request correction, or make a deletion/access request. Some work/attendance records may need to be preserved for lawful operational or recordkeeping purposes.

Do not clear Chrome/Brave extension storage or uninstall while there are pending or unsynchronized task records. Contact your Team Leader or TrinoTime administrator for a safe workflow.

9. Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The publisher intends to use information obtained through browser permissions only to provide or improve the Tracker's disclosed QC features; not for personalized advertising, selling data to brokers, or determining creditworthiness. Access by people is limited to permitted operational purposes, appropriate user consent, security, legal requirements, or appropriately aggregated data.

Use of information obtained from Google APIs, if any, will follow the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.
