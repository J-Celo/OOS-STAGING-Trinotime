Admin Data Management history clearing

Clear Agent History uses CLEAR AGENT HISTORY and previews Agent attendance, Agent OT, Notification Leave requests, Speedtests, Coaching records, and linked Leave overrides. Team Leader overtime requests and approved TL overtime are preserved. In the current Free Pilot deployment these histories are stored in Firebase KV, so the purge deletes only the active KV records and does not target retired direct Leave branches. After the 6-second Undo window, TrinoTime uses a scoped KV delete, retries exact child deletes if needed, and verifies surviving known history when read access is available. The action is blocked while a Speedtest or Coaching session is active. Dated notes, leave balance/ledger records, Team Leader attendance, profiles/accounts, and immutable Admin audit records are preserved. Clear Team Leader Attendance uses CLEAR HISTORY and affects only CWH/Albertsons Team Leader attendance.

Daily welcome and confidence message

After the main dashboard paints, TrinoTime can show a lightweight, non-blocking welcome card with a PHT-aware greeting, emoji, and one of seven rotating work-strength/confidence quotes. Shift Times now has a separate Start and End row for CWH POD 0–12, CWH Onboarding, Tesco, Kroger, and Albertsons POD 0–4. ⏱

Guided attendance and shift-aware punches

Time In, Start Break, End Break, and Time Out follow the required sequence. The active pod shift—or an approved one-day/alternate shift—stamps the attendance record so later schedule changes do not rewrite historical payroll logic.

👔

Team Leader attendance and live status

Team Leaders record Time In, Start Break, End Break, and Time Out from their own account. The Admin Overview separates today's live state from older Past review flags, shows Working / On Break / Not Clocked In / Shift Complete / Scheduled Off counts, and includes the trusted PHT date context. Historical TL records keep the POD stamped when the shift occurred even after a later transfer.

Credited-hours policy: a complete scheduled shift earns up to 6.0 regular hours. The normal pod schedule or an explicitly selected one-day Early shift (10:00 AM–4:30 PM by default) may apply. Late Time In and early Time Out reduce the base credit; an Admin/TL manual deduction may then be applied for documented exceptions such as a power outage or unresponsive/offline time. The first 30 minutes of break are allowed; every excess minute is automatically deducted from regular credited hours. For Albertsons only, Night Differential (ND) is tracked separately for the 10:00 PM–6:00 AM Philippine Time window; each Albertsons shift keeps the ND break rule stamped at Time In, and the ND badge shows whether the result came from Actual punches, Schedule fallback, or a Manual override. Admins and the assigned Team Leader can correct Agent punch times within their authorized POD scope; the same roles can apply an audited manual ND override when needed; ND overrides cannot exceed the actual worked span or 8 hours.
